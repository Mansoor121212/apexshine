Apex Shine / About

Built on Obsession
With the Details

Apex Shine Auto Care didn't start as a business plan. It started with a genuine love for what a perfectly finished vehicle feels like.

Apex Shine Auto Care was founded on a simple belief: your vehicle is more than transportation — it's a reflection of the standard you hold yourself to. That belief shapes every appointment we take on, from a daily commuter to a weekend luxury build.

What began as a personal obsession with paint correction and interior restoration grew into a mobile studio built for Repentigny and the Greater Montreal area. We saw a gap: too many "detailing" services were really just car washes with better marketing. We built Apex Shine to be the opposite — a team that treats every seam, vent, and panel with the same care an owner would give their own vehicle.

Attention to detail isn't a slogan here — it's the actual process. We don't rush jobs to fit more cars into a day. Each vehicle gets a structured, multi-stage process, professional-grade products, and a final inspection before we call it done.

Above all, Apex Shine is committed to a professional client experience: clear communication, honest pricing, and a team that shows up on time and treats your property — and your vehicle — with respect. We're not the cheapest option in Quebec, and we don't want to be. We want to be the one you trust completely.
